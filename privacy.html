Privacy Policy

How Zane Learning Hub handles your information

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: your name and email are saved only in your own browser, on your own device. We do not receive them. The Hub has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The one outside service it uses is Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when fonts load.

1. Who we are

Zane Learning Hub is run by Wilfred Wanyoike. We are responsible for how the Hub handles personal data under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya. You can reach us at mw9770@gmail.com.

2. What the Hub saves

Everything below is saved in your browser’s local storage, on your device:

InformationWhy
Your name and email (your profile)To show your name in the Hub and to record that you created a profile.
Your agreement: the date and the version of the policies you acceptedTo show that you agreed, and to ask again when the policies change materially.
Your progress: modules visited, best quiz scores, flashcard boxes and due dates, weak spots, items seenTo run study mode, weak spots and the daily plan.
Your settings: theme, text size, spacing, font, language, read aloud, offline copyTo keep the Hub looking and working the way you chose.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not send your name, email or progress to any server. We do not receive them, so we cannot see them and we cannot email you.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or profiling.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.

Your email is currently kept only so that you have a complete profile on this device. If a future upgrade needs your email for something else, such as signing in or sending messages, we will update this policy first and ask you to agree again.

4. Outside services and technical data

  • Google Fonts. Each page loads its fonts from Google’s servers. This means Google receives your IP address and basic browser details when a page loads, under Google’s own privacy policy. Google may process this outside Kenya.
  • Hosting. The company that hosts the Hub’s files may keep ordinary server logs (for example IP address, time and the page requested) to keep the service running and secure. We do not use these logs to identify or track you.
  • Read aloud. This uses your browser’s built-in speech feature. Some browsers may use online voices; check your browser’s settings if this matters to you.
  • Live editor. The code you type stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

5. Our basis for handling your data

Your consent. You give it by ticking the box when you create your profile. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your profile (see section 8). The course needs a profile to continue, so withdrawing consent means you will be asked to create one again to keep learning.

6. How long it is kept

Until you delete it or clear your browser data. We hold no copy elsewhere.

7. Keeping it safe

Because your data is in your browser, anyone who can use your browser profile on your device can see it. Please keep your device secure and avoid creating a profile on a shared or public computer.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have rights to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to correct it, to ask for it to be deleted, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Because the data is on your device, you can do most of this yourself on the profile page:

  • See and correct your name and email.
  • Delete your profile, or erase everything (profile, progress and settings) from this device.

For any question or request, email mw9770@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (odpc.go.ke).

9. Children

You must be 18 or older, or have a parent or guardian’s permission, to create a profile. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to read these policies with you first.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is material, the Hub will ask you to agree again before you continue.
