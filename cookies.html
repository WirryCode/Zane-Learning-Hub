Cookies Policy

What the Hub stores in your browser, and why

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: we do not set any cookies. To work properly the Hub uses your browser’s local storage and an offline cache. Both are essential, belong to the Hub only and are never used for tracking.

1. What cookies and similar tools are

Cookies are small files a website can place in your browser. Websites can also use similar tools, such as local storage (a place in your browser where a site saves information) and caches (saved copies of pages so they load faster or work offline).

2. Cookies we set

None. The Hub does not set or read cookies.

3. Similar tools we use

NameKindWhat it doesHow long
hm_profileLocal storageYour name, email and agreement to our policies.Until you delete it
hm_progressLocal storageQuiz scores, flashcard progress, weak spots.Until you delete it
hm_settingsLocal storageTheme, text size, spacing, font, language, read aloud.Until you delete it
Hub cacheService worker cacheSaves pages and files so the Hub loads quickly and works offline.Until a newer version replaces it, or you remove it

All of these are first-party (they belong to the Hub), they stay on your device, and they are needed for the Hub to work as you expect. We do not use them for advertising or tracking.

4. Outside services

The Hub loads fonts from Google Fonts. Requesting them shares your IP address with Google (see the Privacy Policy). We do not add any other outside scripts, analytics or advertising.

5. Why there is no cookie banner

We only use essential storage, so there is nothing optional to switch on or off. If we ever add anything that is not essential, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask your permission first.

6. Managing and removing them

  • On the profile page you can delete your profile or erase everything the Hub has saved in local storage.
  • You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings. This removes local storage and the cache. In the Hub’s Settings you can also remove the offline copy.
